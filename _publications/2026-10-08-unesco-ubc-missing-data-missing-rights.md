**Nazanin Azizmohammadi, Ph.D.**

**Status:** Abstract accepted for presentation; acceptance received on 8 October 2026.  
**Congress:** UNESCO Chair on Health, Race and Human Rights Global Congress 2027.  
**Theme:** Truth-Telling, Human Rights, and Health Justice in an Age of Global Fracture.  
**Organiser:** UNESCO Chair on Health, Race and Human Rights, Peter A. Allard School of Law, University of British Columbia.  
**Congress dates and location:** 1–3 February 2027, San José, Costa Rica.  
**Language:** English.

## Research focus

This contribution examines how gaps in women’s health data can affect AI-supported health decisions in crisis and post-conflict settings, and the implications for women’s right to health.
